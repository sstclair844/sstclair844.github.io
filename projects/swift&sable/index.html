My Process

My work process begins with mindmapping, research and creating a moodboard before sketching out various layouts or logo concepts. I start by writing out visual words associated with the project i'm working on. I then look at other work to find a common theme or at works and what doesn't. Finally I create a moodboard of color palettes, fonts, themed images and more before sketching out various layouts or brand ideas depending on my project.
