About Me

Hello!

My name is Stephanie, and I am a junior graphic and Web designer based in Alton Il. With graphic design i'm able to bring together my lifelong love for art and my newfound love of technology and create engaging visual graphics such as logos, posters, advertisements and more. I am excited to bring energy,a strong work ethic, and creative problem solving to my next project.

My Process

My work process begins with mindmapping, research and creating a moodboard before sketching out various layouts or logo concepts. I start by writing out visual words associated with the project i'm working on. I then look at other work to find a common theme or what works and what doesn't. Finally I create a moodboard of color palettes, fonts, themed images and more before sketching out various layouts or brand ideas depending on my project.
