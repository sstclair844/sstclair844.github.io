Midnight Drive

The Story

Reader Beware, Your'e In For A Scare....

You're a trucker driving on an empty desert highway in the dead of night, it’s almost pitch-black, the only light is from the headlights on your truck.
All around you is a desolate void of darkness cast by a starless sky. You’re behind on schedule and short on sleep, up ahead in the distance
you see the only other sign of life on this vacant road, a woman of all things, standing there as if she’s waiting for a ride.

What Do You Do?

Do You Stop? Do You Keep Driving?
